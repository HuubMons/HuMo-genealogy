# Features

## Genealogy website features

HuMo-genealogy is built to support a complete online family tree website.

### Included capabilities

- GEDCOM import from many genealogy applications
- Family tree presentation for persons and families
- Optional showing of sources, media and texts
- Control over access to living persons
- Support for multiple languages
- Custom layouts and CSS styling
- Expansion with extra pages and custom content

### Designed for flexibility

The software is intended for hobbyists and genealogists who want a personal site with direct control over the content and structure of their family tree.

It is especially useful when you want to:

- publish a family tree online
- keep your data under your own control
- share it with relatives or selected visitors
- customize the public presentation with your own design
